# CURIOUS REALITY — TOPIC MAP

## STATUS

Document Type: Topic Taxonomy, Discovery Map & Knowledge Relationship System
Brand: Curious Reality
Authority: Derived from CURIOUS_REALITY_MASTER_ARCHITECTURE.md

Purpose:

Define the broad knowledge territory of Curious Reality without locking the brand into a single niche.

This document organizes possible subject areas so that humans and AI systems can:

- discover new questions;
- avoid repetitive coverage;
- identify content gaps;
- connect related subjects;
- discover interdisciplinary opportunities;
- organize the knowledge archive;
- plan future videos;
- build long-term topic authority.

The subject categories are navigation structures, not permanent restrictions.

The brand follows QUESTIONS, not categories.

---

# 1. CORE TOPIC PHILOSOPHY

Curious Reality does not have one narrow content niche.

The stable identity is:

QUESTION-FIRST KNOWLEDGE.

Any subject can become Curious Reality content when it contains a compelling question worth understanding.

Therefore:

Topic Category
≠
Brand Identity

The topic is the environment.

The question is the content identity.

# 33. TOPIC CATEGORY: MYSTERIES

## Definition

Subjects involving unresolved questions, unusual events, unexplained observations, or incomplete evidence.

## Rule

Mystery does not mean fiction.

For real-world mysteries:

- state what is known;
- state what is unknown;
- identify evidence;
- identify competing explanations;
- preserve uncertainty.

Do not manufacture certainty for dramatic effect.

# 36. QUESTION CLUSTERS

A single topic should generate multiple questions.

Example:

TOPIC:
Smartphone Camera

Possible Questions:

How does a tiny camera capture detail?

Why does the image look different from what our eyes see?

How does the sensor detect color?

Why does low light create noise?

How does computational photography improve the image?

What happens between pressing the shutter and seeing the final photo?

The topic becomes a question cluster rather than one isolated video.

# 37. TOPIC DEPTH MODEL

Each topic can exist at multiple depths.

LEVEL 1 — OBSERVATION

What does the viewer notice?

LEVEL 2 — SIMPLE EXPLANATION

What is the immediate reason?

LEVEL 3 — MECHANISM

How does the system actually work?

LEVEL 4 — DEEP STRUCTURE

What happens underneath?

LEVEL 5 — CONNECTION

How does this connect to another system?

LEVEL 6 — IMPLICATION

Why does this matter?

This allows one topic to produce both Shorts and long-form content.

# 39. TOPIC CLUSTER RULE

Do not publish multiple videos that answer essentially the same question without meaningful new value.

Before creating a new video, ask:

Has Curious Reality already answered this?

If yes:

Can the new video provide:

- a different question;
- deeper mechanism;
- new evidence;
- a different context;
- a new comparison;
- a new visual explanation;
- or a meaningful follow-up?

If not, prefer a different topic.

# 40. CONTENT GAP DETECTION

AI should regularly inspect the topic map and ask:

Which major topic areas have no content?

Which questions are repeatedly unanswered?

Which concepts have only surface-level coverage?

Which related subjects are disconnected?

# 43. TRENDING TOPICS

Trending subjects may be used when the trend contains a genuine Curious Reality question.

Do not create a trend video merely because the subject is popular.

Ask:

What is actually worth understanding here?

A trend should be the entry point.

The explanation should remain the core product.

---

# 44. CURRENT-EVENT TOPICS

Current events can be used when:

- the event genuinely contains an important question;
- reliable evidence exists;
- context can be provided;
- the topic fits the brand.

Do not turn Curious Reality into a general news channel.

The content should answer or investigate a meaningful question.

# 49. TOPIC REJECTION RULE

Reject or reframe topics that are:

- purely generic trivia;
- unsupported;
- duplicate content;
- weakly explainable;
- impossible to source;
- misleading;
- primarily chosen for trend value;
- lacking a meaningful payoff;
- dependent on fabricated mystery.

---

# 50. TOPIC MAP USAGE BY AI

When generating content ideas, the AI should:

1. Identify the strongest category.
2. Identify neighboring categories.
3. Search for an interesting question.
4. Check the existing archive.
5. Avoid duplicate coverage.
6. Identify evidence requirements.
7. Identify visual opportunities.
8. Identify possible follow-up questions.
9. Estimate whether the idea fits a Short or long-form format.
10. Add the idea to the appropriate question cluster.

---
